🗺️ Utility Measures — How They Feed Domain Measures

These measures do NOT appear directly on any visual. They are the invisible denominators and building blocks inside every rate formula.
Encounter Count
# Total Encounters
Used by: ALL rate measures (e.g., Readmission %, FWA %)
Patient-Days
# Total Patient Days
Used by: HAI, Safety Incident per-1k-pt-days measures
Patient Count
# Unique Patients
Used by: Prevalence calculations, HEDIS rates
Nurse Count
# Unique Nurses
Used by: Burnout %, Turnover %, Overtime per nurse
📊 Revenue Funnel Chart (Financial page)
$ Total Submitted Charges → Gross billing (top of funnel)
$ Total Allowed Revenue → Net receivable (contracted)
📋 Financial KPI Table
$ Total Margin Estimate → Operating margin row
⚙️ Key DAX Pattern
Rate = DIVIDE( COUNTX(…flag=1), [# Total Encounters] )
